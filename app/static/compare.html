    Create a new merged folder

    Copies one of everything into a new folder. The folders you compared aren't changed at all. Safest, and needs space for one more copy.

    Merge into folder A

    Copies whatever folder A is missing into it (versions and clashing names get “(2)”-style suffixes; nothing in A is replaced). Then you can move the leftover copies to the Trash.
