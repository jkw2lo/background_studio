Background Studio
Remove backgroundsCut out the subject of each picture
Remove backgrounds

Cuts the subject out of your pictures and saves them as new files, on a transparent background or a colour of your choice. Works on whole folders, and you can fine-tune any picture by hand.

Good for: product photos, portraits, scanned stickers and stamps. Your originals are never changed.

Sort & tagPut pictures into categories
Sort & tag

Go through a pile of pictures and give each one a category with a single key press, helped by AI suggestions that run on your Mac. Then copy them into a folder per category, add Finder tags or export a spreadsheet.

Good for: trip photos, collections, anything that needs organising. Your pictures stay where they are.

Compare & mergeFind duplicates, combine folders
Compare & merge

Shows what's the same and what's different across two or more folders, including exact duplicates, re-saved copies and edited versions. Then merges them into one folder with one of everything.

Good for: a folder and its backup, or copies scattered across folders. Nothing changes until you choose to merge.
